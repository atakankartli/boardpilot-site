Board Pilot privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

We collect no data

Board Pilot does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your server

The app talks only to the Board Pilot server address that you enter. That server is yours. The data you see (projects, board files, session status) stays between your phone and your server.

On your phone

The server address and token are saved in the iOS keychain on your device. Delete the app to remove them.
